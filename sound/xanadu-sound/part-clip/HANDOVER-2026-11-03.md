## 1. What is done

### 1.1 The idea (Bekir, 31 Oct – 1 Nov) [📜]

- A video clip for Arvo Pärt's ***Für Alina*** — Alexander Malter, ECM *Alina* (1999), track 2, **10:47, piano only**. Later, maybe, *Spiegel im Spiegel* and *Cantus in memoriam Benjamin Britten*.
- A surface seen from above carrying a woman's profile, half-seen. Every note is an impact on the surface; where it lands, the picture changes a little and never returns. "The image of a woman who cannot be reached."
- Three axes, **never mixed in one piece**: **medium** (liquid / solid / granular) · **layer** (up to four images, each with its own height map, then white) · **impact** (drop, strike, sweep, blow, flow, + scratch — four numbers each: energy, area, duration, direction).
- Rulings in force: *Für Alina* = **liquid**; *Cantus* = sweep (granular, advisor's view); the image **is** the sand in the granular medium ("the pixels are grains"); layers stay four; erosion bottoms out at white; drops do not start by themselves (events or taps); stay in WebGL, Blender only if the 1080p test render fails the eye; **nothing public until Universal Edition answers**.

### 1.2 The prototype — eight passes, all reviewed, all pushed [☀️]

Method on every pass: Sonnet writes to a scoped brief → Opus reviews independently (never saw it written) → the advisor looks at rendered frames → push. Liquid and solid are pixel-identical across all granular passes (10-scene identity set, 0.0000 / 0).

| Pass | Date | What it added |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | 1 Nov | light panel (azimuth, elevation, intensity, Kelvin, ambient), erosion field 512², thin layer edge, per-layer thicknesses, Molten gold and Lead liquids |
| 1b | 1 Nov | any aspect ratio, fit cover/contain/stretch, RENDER settings block, PNG/JPEG/TIFF upload (inline TIFF decoder, 16-bit → half-float) |
| 1c | 1 Nov | texture slots per surface, save/load settings JSON, impact model first half (drop + strike), Random 45 s mode |
| 2 | 1 Nov | liquid strike rebuilt (zero-volume swell), per-layer height maps, relief steers wear and cracks |
| 3a | 1 Nov | third family **granular** (Sand, Dust): deposit field, crater + conserving rim + thrown grains, angle-of-repose relaxation, top-grain colour rule (grains never blend) |
| 3a-look | 1–2 Nov | rim colour by removed volume, RGBA32F deposit (16F fallback), bed colour dithered by nearest layer, 24 small irregular discs, volume accounting (bottom-out, wear, 5th strike) |
| 3b-i | 2–3 Nov | **wind on granular** (`#impact=blow`: band gust, stochastic hops, exact conservation, bed lift; sliders `windLift` / `windCarry` / `windBed`), repose gate on motion (stops when nothing moves), strike rim colour by depth quantile, grain boundary at full thickness |

What the prototype can do today: load up to four of Bekir's images with height maps, choose a medium and a light, receive drop / strike / blow events (synthetic, tapped, or Random 45 s), run deterministically (same seed → byte-identical frames), save and load its whole state as JSON. URL hooks: `#surface=`, `#impact=`, `#aspect=`, `#auto=1`, `#seek=N`, `#random=1&seed=N`.

What it cannot do yet: sweep, flow, scratch; clods; read a real `events.json` from the recording; render offline to a PNG sequence; play audio (by design — rights).

## 2. What is to do

In order. Owner in brackets. Items 1 and 2 gate the rest: a sand route Bekir rejects on the iPad should not get more passes.

### 2.1 [Bekir] Judge the granular medium on the iPad — unanswered since 2 Nov [☁️]

No real GPU has run passes 3a → 3b-i; every number comes from SwiftShader. Scenario, one word per answer:
1. `#surface=sand` — tap one drop: **crater or ring?** Three drops on one spot: does the pile read as sand?
2. `#surface=sand&impact=strike` — the dark rim: **thrown sand or a flat disc?** Are the small discs grains or ink?
3. `#surface=sand&impact=blow` (or Random 45 s on Sand) — a default gust: **wind over sand, or the picture dissolving into speckle?** (Measured: one default blow recolours 12.3 % of the pixels while the bed loses only 0.0056 — a one-grain film repaints a pixel. Levers ready: smaller default `area`, lower `windBed`, or a thin-film display rule.) Does the lee smear behind a crater read as a drift?
4. Dust vs Sand — does Dust earn its place?
5. **Frame rate** from the HUD: while a pile slides (the repose gate reads the GPU back every 0.25 s — Chrome warns on SwiftShader; unknown on Apple GPUs), and during a gust.
6. Is the top image's full thickness right (the lower layer shows only in the last fifth of a layer)?

Also still open from earlier passes: thin lip vs old bevel on the liquid edge; strike basin amplitude (0.30); default layer thicknesses (advisor suggested [0.4, 1.5, 2.5, 3]); `reliefCrack` strength with his own height maps.

### 2.2 [Advisor → Sonnet/Opus] Pass 3b-ii — brief not written [☁️ design in PLAN-v2 A9]

- **Clods**: three nested scales in one RGB id texture cut once per seed; cohesion rising with clod size; `clodFraction` slider; borders invisible until a clod breaks.
- **`events.json` set-events**: `{t, type:"set", granular.live:{…}, ramp}` so a piece can change wind / repose / grain mid-way.
- From the 3b-i flags: noise on the wind band's edge (a narrow blow's edge is a ruler line); whole-canvas speckle lever per Bekir's answer in 2.1; ghost rim (a second deposit peak 36–48 texels downwind — more or log-spaced hop classes); plume streaks (lateral ±2); strike rim relief (`GRAN_SHADE_K` or a taller strike rim); HUD "drops" count naming blows; `fnote` wording.
- Then **3c**: particles for flying patches.
- Rule of the house for the brief: one 1024 "look frame" **before** the full test battery. Three of the advisor's briefs were rewritten after the frames were seen (the first wind was a conveyor, not hops); the look frame is cheaper than a second writer round.

### 2.3 Passes 4–6 (PLAN-v2 §C, IMPACTS "Passes")

- **Pass 4** — sweep for all three media: absorbs along the path, **piles at the sweep's edge** (ruling: accumulates, never vanishes). The natural impact for *Cantus* (each string group one sweep at its own speed, same direction; bell = drop/strike).
- **Pass 5** — blow and flow for liquid and solid (blow pushes flakes → plates with energy; flow drags the image itself). **Scratch** after this, or earlier if a piece needs it — Bekir must first say what the point does to each medium (IMPACTS has only the advisor's reading).
- **B5** — dispersive waves for the liquid (short ripples faster than long swells). Opus, isolated, revertible.

### 2.4 B2 + B3 — the render path, and the Blender decision [☁️]

- **B3** `events.json` from the Malter recording: onset detection (librosa, Python) on the ECM track → `{t, pitch, vel}` → pitch → position, velocity → energy, with the drop rule for *Für Alina* (~15–20 notes/min; strike for the rare heavy chords). Hand correction of onsets expected — the piece is quiet and sparse.
- **B2** `render.js`: pinned headless Chrome steps the sim deterministically from `events.json` → PNG sequence → ffmpeg. 1080p / 25 fps first; 4K is the target.
- **Decision point:** the first 60 s at 1080p, watched by Bekir. If it reads as a "screen effect", only the look moves to Blender (Dynamic Paint + Cycles); rules and events stay JSON.
- Audio is laid in afterwards (Resolve); the prototype never plays sound.

### 2.5 [Bekir + Advisor] Rights — the real gate [☁️]

1. Bekir supplies: venues/dates, edition size (skeleton says 5 + 2 AP), online yes/no, one still, one paragraph on himself, **the concept paragraph in his own words** (he said he will dictate it; do not write it for him).
2. Advisor drafts the UE letter in **two options** (as agreed), verifies the *Cantus* catalogue number and UE's current licensing contact route, and lists the two works (*Alina*, *Cantus*).
3. In parallel: ECM master quote for *Alina* (ECM 1591, track 2) **or** the price of a newly commissioned recording of comparable slowness.
4. Until the answer: private study only; no social posts, no exhibition listing.

### 2.6 Known risks the next person should hold in mind

- **No real-GPU run yet.** iOS-specific unknowns: mixed-format MRT (RGBA32F + R8), the PBO readback of the repose gate, 32-bit float render targets (`readMask()` returns zeros without them; a failure refuses the granular family cleanly). Three 2048² 16-bit textures ≈ 170 MB on an iPad — keep textures at 1024 on mobile.
- **The look is not yet approved by Bekir.** Every pass since 3a shipped "with flags"; the advisor judged frames, Bekir has not. A rejection at 2.1 changes the order of 2.2–2.3 (sweep for *Cantus* could come before clods).
- **The liquid medium is where *Alina* lives**, and it has had the least attention since pass 2. B5 (dispersive waves) is the one liquid item that changes how the clip reads; don't let the granular work push it past the render test.
- **Perfectionism vs shipping:** the prototype is already good enough to make the 60-s render test. The advisor's view: do B3 + B2 right after 3b-ii, before passes 4–5 — the render test is the decision that tells us whether any of the remaining passes are worth their cost.
