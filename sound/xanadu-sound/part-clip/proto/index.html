Relief (the height maps: light, wear, cracks)

      

      

      

      
Layer bump: the relief of each layer's own height map (a solid shades it like the bump; a liquid shows it faintly through the film, on the image only). Relief wear: ridges (light) erode faster, valleys (dark) slower, in both families. Relief crack (solids): cracks lean toward valleys. All three read the layer at the surface and, on a solid, the medium's height texture; with no height map loaded they change nothing.

      
relief crack steers the cracks of solid surfaces only
